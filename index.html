About Me

Hey everyone, I’m Cullen. I’m a junior majoring in Fine Arts with a focus on videography, and I’m planning to graduate in 2028. After school, I want to work in the action sports filming industry.

Some of my biggest passions include skiing, snowmobiling, watersports, and backcountry guiding. Spending time outdoors plays a huge role in the kind of work I want to create.

The main thing I’m hoping to get out of this class is feeling more confident and transferable when it comes to designing websites. I want to run my own business someday, so learning these skills feels really important.

Games, Community, & Problem Solving

One of the things that really draws me to games is the sense of community and problem solving. I think it’s amazing that you can play with almost anyone, no matter where they are in the world.

I also enjoy that many of the games I play test your ability to solve problems and communicate effectively. One example of this is the We Were Here Forever franchise, which completely depends on teamwork, communication, and trust.

For me, games are at their best when they bring people together and encourage collaboration.
